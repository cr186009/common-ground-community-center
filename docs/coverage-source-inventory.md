# Coverage source inventory

Verified: 2026-08-13. This is a discovery backlog only; nothing here is wired into scraper runtime.

## Evaluation key

- **P0**: implement after the existing scraper fleet is healthy.
- **P1**: valuable follow-up or requires endpoint discovery/deduplication work.
- **P2**: enrichment, seasonal, low-yield, or high-overlap source.
- **Evidence** describes whether the source supplies an authoritative title, date/time, venue, and stable detail URL.
- Before implementation, recheck `robots.txt` and terms, use an identifiable user agent, cache responses, and avoid bypassing authentication or technical controls.

## Recommended implementation sequence

1. Stabilize existing primary scrapers first.
2. Add Canton City master, CCSD iCal, Play Cherokee, Chamber, and NWS alerts.
3. Add Kennesaw City master, Cobb Schools ICS, North Cobb Library, NWS alerts, and filtered KSU Localist.
4. Add WGRLS with a strict Paulding-branch allowlist.
5. Reuse platform adapters: WordPress/Tribe (Acworth, WGRLS, Play Cherokee), CivicEngage (Paulding, Marietta), Granicus/Legistar, Finalsite iCal, and Revize.
6. Only then add enrichment feeds with explicit source precedence and cross-source dedupe rules.

## Pre-implementation checklist

- Recheck the exact URL, payload, robots policy, and visible terms on implementation day.
- Record a stable upstream ID/UID, canonical detail URL, source organization, retrieval timestamp, and raw date/time evidence.
- Define city assignment from an exact venue/address allowlist; do not infer city only from the publisher's service area.
- Preserve cancellations, updates, timezone, recurrence, all-day semantics, and alert expiry.
- Establish source precedence before enabling overlapping master/subset or city/tourism feeds.
- Fixture-test pagination, empty calendars, malformed events, recurrence, and source layout changes.

### Cross-check conclusions

- The strongest omissions from the original adjacent-area table are the Paulding Chamber ICS, the PCSD district/school calendar family, Cobb Schools Acworth ICS, Dallas civic meetings, and Rockmart's distinct civic versus arts pages.
- Do not add separate scrapers for City masters and their linked tourism/venue subsets until source precedence and cross-source deduplication are fixture-tested.
- Treat Visit Woodstock as a broad aggregator with official-DMO standing, not as authoritative for every submitted merchant event; retain the underlying organizer and canonical detail URL.
- Treat Marietta.com as discovery only, North Cobb Regional Library as Kennesaw rather than Acworth, and Hiram vendor PDFs as stale artifacts rather than feeds.
- The Cedartown robots rule is a hard hold. Missing or empty robots responses for Woodstock, Rockmart and Polk County are unresolved access evidence, not approval.
